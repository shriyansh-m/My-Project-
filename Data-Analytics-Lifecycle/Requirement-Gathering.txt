The requirements and gathering in the data analytics lifecycle involve several key stages:

Problem Definition: Clearly define the business problem and objectives to ensure 
the analytics project is focused and relevant. 

Requirements Gathering: Engage stakeholders through interviews, surveys, and workshops 
to gather detailed requirements and expectations. 

Data Discovery: Investigate data sources and develop preliminary hypotheses 
that can be tested with data. 

Data Preparation: Clean and preprocess data to prepare it for analysis, 
ensuring quality and accuracy. 

Model Planning: Explore data relationships and select suitable models for analysis. 

These stages are crucial for aligning the analytics project with business goals 
and ensuring actionable insights are generated.
