The Data Analytics Lifecycle is a structured approach to solving data-driven problems. 
When focusing on descriptive statistics, the lifecycle emphasizes understanding, 
preparing, and summarizing data to extract meaningful insights. 
Below are the relevant phases tailored to descriptive statistics:

Discovery Phase This phase involves understanding the problem and identifying 
the data sources required for analysis. The team formulates initial hypotheses and 
gathers context about the data. For descriptive statistics, this step ensures 
clarity on what needs to be summarized or described.

Data Preparation Phase Data is explored, cleaned, and preprocessed to ensure it is 
ready for analysis. This includes handling missing values, removing duplicates, 
and transforming data into a usable format. Tools like OpenRefine or 
Hadoop may assist in this phase. For descriptive statistics, this ensures 
the data is accurate and consistent for summarization.

Model Planning Phase In this phase, relationships between variables 
are explored, and key variables are selected. For descriptive statistics, 
this involves calculating measures like mean, median, mode, standard deviation, 
and identifying patterns or trends in the data.

Communication of Results The findings from descriptive statistics are summarized and 
presented to stakeholders. This includes visualizations like histograms, 
bar charts, and summary tables to convey insights effectively. 
The goal is to articulate the key patterns and trends observed in the data.

Descriptive statistics primarily focus on summarizing and understanding the 
data in the early stages of the lifecycle. It provides the foundation for 
deeper analysis in subsequent phases.
