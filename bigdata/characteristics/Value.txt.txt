VALUE
The value of Big Data lies in its potential to derive meaningful insights and create value for organisations. By analysing large and diverse datasets, organisations can uncover Big Data Patterns, correlations, and trends that can lead to valuable insights.

These insights, in turn, can drive informed decision-making, optimise business processes, improve customer experiences, and foster innovation. The value of Big Data lies not only in its quantity but also in the quality of the insights derived from it.
